# Training-data licence register

Audit date: 2026-11-18 (Mendeley rows re-verified through DataCite on 2026-11-20). Purpose: record, before any model is trained, which datasets may legally be used, under which
terms, and what was actually verified. **"Verified" means the licence was read on a primary page on the audit date.**
Anything else is marked unverified and must not be used for training until a person confirms it.

This register is separate from `datasets/catalog.json` (which drives the app's Datasets page): candidate datasets are not
shown to farmers.

## Rules

| Licence | Use for training? | Obligation |
|---|---|---|
| CC0, CC BY 4.0, Apache-2.0 | Yes | Attribute (not needed for CC0); keep the DOI/licence text with the data and the model card |
| CC BY-SA (any version) | Only with a recorded decision | Share-alike may extend to derivative data and possibly to models trained on it. Decide before use |
| Not stated, conflicting, or only seen on a mirror | **No** | Confirm on the primary record first |
| NC (non-commercial) | No (this app is intended for real farmers) | — |

A permissive licence is not a privacy or deployment clearance: no personal data, GPS of private fields or faces may be
published or redistributed with a model.

## Weights

Models are trained from random initialisation unless a pretrained checkpoint's licence is confirmed. ImageNet-pretrained
weights are not downloaded by default because the ImageNet terms are research-oriented and their use in a product is a
separate decision for the owner.

## Honest limits

* Public datasets are from other seasons, regions and cameras. A score on their held-out test split is **not** the
  accuracy farmers will see. The only meaningful number is accuracy on photos from the app's own fields, labelled by an
  agronomist (the consent + reviewer workflow already exists in the database).
* Disease cannot be read from satellite NDVI. Satellite datasets (AgriFieldNet) can support crop type and growth-stage
  checks, not diagnosis.

## Licence clarification from official/public primary records (checked 2026-11-27)

* **Government Open Data License (GODL) – India:** The official data.gov.in licence grants worldwide, royalty-free use, adaptation, publication and derivative works for lawful commercial and non-commercial purposes, subject to attribution and other conditions. It applies only when the relevant data provider actually offers the specific dataset under GODL and has authority to license it. A government-hosted PDF or government-funded data is not automatically GODL. Verify the individual dataset landing-page licence before relying on GODL. Source: https://data.gov.in/Godl
* **PlantVillage:** The linked original-author Hugging Face card currently states CC BY-SA 3.0, while a separate standardized mirror states CC BY 3.0 and the original GitHub repo has no licence file. This conflict means the dataset is not approved for redistribution or CHM deployment until the controlling licence and authority over all photos are confirmed. Sources: https://huggingface.co/datasets/mohanty/PlantVillage ; https://github.com/ai-agriculture-circuits-and-systems/plant_village ; https://github.com/spMohanty/PlantVillage-Dataset
* **PlantDoc:** The original repo declares CC BY 4.0, but its README says images were collected from internet searches. A repo-level licence does not establish image-by-image rights. Keep blocked for redistribution/deployment unless underlying photo rights are cleared. Source: https://github.com/pratikkayal/PlantDoc-Dataset
* **Kaggle Paddy Doctor competition data:** The competition rules state Competition Data may be used for academic research and education, and the download is subject to CC BY 4.0. However, the same rules prohibit transmitting, duplicating, publishing, redistributing or otherwise making Competition Data available to anyone who has not formally agreed to the rules. Keep downloaded competition files private to eligible participants; do not commit raw data to a public repository. This is distinct from the IEEE DataPort Paddy Doctor dataset above. Sources: https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/paddy-disease-classification/rules ; https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/paddy-disease-classification/data
